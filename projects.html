BEMC "Copper Fangs" PCB Robot

2026

I led a team of 4 in the Bright Electronics Manufacturing Challenge, a national PCB design and build competition, and contributed to PCB design, code, and assembly. We designed the robot in Altium Designer around a PIC18F26Q43, with fuzzy logic control across 7 IR sensors and a custom circuit that boosts a 5V input to a 7.1V output. We placed 5th nationally in the design round, were 1 of 10 national finalists from 31 teams, and reached the race quarterfinals. When a part shorted the night before we flew out, I made a backup plan: overnighting a replacement to the hotel and having equivalent parts bought as insurance.

  • Altium Designer
  • PIC18
  • Fuzzy Logic

Permafrost Thaw Undergraduate Research

Spring 2025, Spring Research Fellowship

With a classmate and faculty advisor Dr. Kaitlin Hill, I modeled permafrost thaw by coupling the Budyko energy balance model with a 1D heat transfer equation. I built and ran the Budyko model in Python, and we simulated the system with Euler and finite difference methods. The work was presented at the 2025 Texas Undergraduate Mathematics Conference and the St. Mary's Spring Research Showcase.

  • Python
  • Numerical Methods
  • Climate Modeling

About Me

I am a dedicated and ambitious Computer Engineering student at St. Mary’s University, passionate about technology, problem-solving, and innovation. My expertise includes Java, C, Python, and C# programming, and I thrive in developing technical solutions and optimizing processes.

I have hands-on experience through internships with the City of San Antonio and Accenture Federal Services, where I provided IT support, managed technical assets, and contributed to traffic system optimizations. Beyond academics, I am actively involved in the Society of Hispanic Professional Engineers (SHPE), the Honors Program, and research initiatives.

I enjoy working on personal projects, including robotics, electronics, and automotive modifications. My strong leadership skills, adaptability, and work ethic drive me to excel in both collaborative and independent settings.

About Me

I'm Nicholas Galus, a computer engineering student at St. Mary's University with a passion for bridging hardware and software through embedded systems. Coming from a culturally diverse background — Hispanic, Filipino, and Ukrainian — I've always been shaped by different perspectives and a strong hands-on approach. From early experiences helping with LEGO robotics and rebuilding engines, I developed a curiosity for how systems work and a drive to improve them. My career goal is to design and optimize technologies like engine control modules and automation systems that connect mechanical performance with software precision.
